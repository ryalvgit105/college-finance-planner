### 4. Functionality: Persistence / Month-Propagation
- **Status:** FAIL / GAP
- **Observation:** The item added in January ("TestPizza") did NOT appear when switching to February. The budget for February remained at the default $590.00.
- **Implication:** The current implementation treats budget edits as strictly local to the specific month being edited. There is no automatic carry-over or "apply to future months" feature. This may be unintuitive for users who expect a budget plan to be annual.

### Recommendations
- Implement a checkbox in the "Edit Budget" modal to "Apply to all future months" or "Apply to 2025".
- Alternatively, initialize new months with the previous month's budget data.
